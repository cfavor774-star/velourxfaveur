The Studio

Texture, mood,
and a little grain.

VelourXfaveur is built around one idea: a photograph should feel like something you could touch. We work in natural light, real film grain, and honest moments — favoring atmosphere over polish.

Every shoot starts with a conversation about the feeling you want people to have when they see the final images, then we build the shot list backward from there.
